Why Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) Save UAE Properties Up to 35%

  1. Home
  2. Blogs
  3. AMC Savings
September 2026 Deoraj Engineering Dept Facility Maintenance

Operating residential towers or commercial properties in the UAE comes with a distinct climate challenge: prolonged peak temperatures exceeding 45°C coupled with high coastal humidity. In this environment, relying on reactive "fix-it-when-it-breaks" repairs leads to catastrophic equipment failures and exorbitant costs.

1. The High Cost of Emergency Breakdown Calls

When an HVAC chiller fails during July or August, the cost is rarely limited to the technician’s bill. Property owners face tenant disruption, severe humidity damage to interior gyprock ceilings, and emergency premium surcharges for rush parts procurement. With an active Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC), routine coil pressure washing and refrigerant gas audits eliminate over 80% of preventable summer breakdowns.

2. Energy Efficiency & DEWA/ADDC Consumption

A dust-clogged condenser coil forces compressors to operate under higher head pressures, increasing power consumption by 15% to 30%. Routine quarterly chemical cleansing and airflow balancing ensure maximum thermodynamic heat transfer, directly reducing monthly utility bills.

Key Engineering Takeaway:

For every 1 AED invested in structured preventative maintenance and scheduled filter changes, property managers avoid an estimated 3.8 AED in premature component replacement and emergency HVAC rental chillers.

3. Extended Asset Lifecycle

Capital equipment such as water booster pumps, central ducted split units, and electrical main distribution boards (MDBs) are designed to last 15 to 20 years. Without routine thermal scans and mechanical lubrication, life expectancy drops to under 8 years, accelerating capital depreciation.
