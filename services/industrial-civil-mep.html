Specialized Heavy Industrial Civil & Plant MEP Engineering

Deoraj Contracting and General Maintenance - LLC delivers heavy industrial civil foundations, machine foundation grouting, plant substations, industrial drainage, and comprehensive Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) infrastructure for industrial facilities across the UAE.

Our specialized teams operate in petrochemical plants, industrial processing units, manufacturing facilities, and utility substations adhering to strict oil & gas safety guidelines, permit-to-work (PTW) protocols, and UAE municipal standards.

Scope of Capabilities

Heavy Equipment Foundations

High-tolerance concrete foundations for heavy rotating equipment, compressors, turbine bases, transformers, and industrial vibratory machinery.

Industrial MEP Systems

High and low-voltage electrical distribution, explosion-proof fittings, industrial cable tray networks, and industrial HVAC chillers.

Plant Drainage & Trenches

Chemical-resistant industrial trenches, oily water separators, interceptor pits, and containment bund walls for chemical storage.

Precision Grouting & Anchoring

Non-shrink cementitious and epoxy grouting for structural baseplates, anchor bolt installations, and alignment leveling.
