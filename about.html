Who We Are

Engineering Precision & Heavy Industrial Capability Across the UAE

Deoraj Contracting and General Maintenance - LLC - has established a prominent reputation as a multi-disciplinary contractor delivering heavy industrial civil infrastructure, building construction, process piping, thermal insulation, refractory, scaffolding, and specialized manpower services throughout the United Arab Emirates.

With certified capabilities spanning oil & gas support, petrochemical processing units, commercial real estate, and municipal infrastructure, our engineers and qualified workforce maintain uncompromising standards in execution speed, quality compliance, and personnel safety.

From routine building maintenance and facilities AMC contracts to complex turnaround shutdowns involving boiler refractory overhauls, GRP/FRP pipe lamination, and heavy earthworks, we ensure end-to-end reliability.
